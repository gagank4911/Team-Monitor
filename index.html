Team settings

Set a different number of leave days for one person in the "Leave taken / allowed + carried" column above; leave it empty to use the default. Unused paid leave carries into the next year, up to the max above; the carried number is worked out automatically, and you can type a different one for a person (for example their leftover from before the app was used). Max away per day: once this many people have asked for leave or WFH on a date, anyone else who asks gets a warning and their request is flagged in red.

Sundays are off. Each Saturday one group works, in turn (1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1); everyone else is off. Set each person's group in the Team overview table above.

Check-in locations are compared with this. An "Office" check-in farther away is shown in red. Leave empty to only show each location on a map.
Days under the expected hours are flagged in timesheets.
Team members and managers are set in config.js and firestore.rules. Members can check in, request leave, assign tasks to anyone and update their own tasks, and see the team calendar (who is on leave or working from home) but only their own check-in locations. Managers can see everyone's check-in locations, approve leave, assign tasks to anyone, plan team bonding and change these settings.
