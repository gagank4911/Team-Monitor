Add a teammate

Everyone in config.js is put on the board automatically. To add a new person, add their name and email to config.js and firestore.rules first; to bring back someone you removed, search for them here.

Team settings

Set a different number of leave days for one person in the "Leave taken / allowed" column above; leave it empty to use the default. Max away per day: once this many people have asked for leave or WFH on a date, anyone else who asks gets a warning and their request is flagged in red.

Sundays are off. Each Saturday one group works, in turn (1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1); everyone else is off. Set each person's group in the Team overview table above.

Days under the expected hours are flagged in timesheets.
Team members and managers are set in config.js and firestore.rules. Members can check in, request leave and update their own tasks, and see only their own leave requests. Managers can see the team calendar, approve leave, assign tasks to anyone, plan team bonding and change these settings.
